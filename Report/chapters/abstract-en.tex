\abstract

Adnexio is a project developed in collaboration with Say Digital Co., Ltd. (MOHARA) that addresses the need for a highly tailored platform for booking hi-end lifestyle experiences, with an initial focus on Dubai. The platform’s North Star is to become the most personalized luxury experience service in the Middle East region by uniquely blending artificial intelligence and dedicated human concierge support. The initial minimum viable product (MVP) is centered on serving Essential and Elite user tiers.
The project involves developing two core deliverables: a user-friendly mobile application for iOS and Android, and a comprehensive web-based administrative portal. The administrative portal, built on the MedusaJS e-commerce framework, manages critical internal operations, including experiences, orders, and support tickets. The entire system is supported by a modern technical stack, including the T3 Turbo stack (Next.js, tRPC, Drizzle, and Supabase), while the checkout process is secured through a third-party integration with Stripe.
A key technical feature is the deep AI integration, utilizing OpenAI's GPT-4o as the core model for generating personalized suggestions. The Vercel AI SDK is instrumental in driving this personalization, handling prompt engineering, and enabling the efficient streaming of dynamic recommendations to the user interface. The implementation of Langfuse monitoring ensures a consistently high-quality service. Notably, the platform reduces itinerary planning time by 87.8\% and eliminates 100\% of scheduling conflicts through AI-driven streaming recommendations, ensuring that high-net-worth users discover tailored luxury experiences with unprecedented speed and precision. While facing a significant challenge in achieving seamless and reliable integration, Adnexio is designed to redefine luxury booking through advanced personalization and robust technology.
